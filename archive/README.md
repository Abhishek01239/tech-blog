# Archive

Articles in this folder are **not built or published**. They were removed from `content/posts/` during the Google AdSense readiness pass on 2026-10-26.

## Why they are here

- **56 placeholder-title posts** (2026-08-08 → 2026-08-17) have 2–3 word generated titles ("Apple Revamps", "Google Code", "Log4j Hit") and repeat a handful of stories — Dart 3 three times, iOS 17 four times, Pixel 8 twice, Google fined twice. Low-value duplicate pages are the main reason AdSense rejects a site.
- **16 near-duplicate stories** published days apart with nearly identical titles, several with contradictory facts (Llama 3.0 70B vs 3.2 vs 10B; EU fines of $1.2B / $2.4B / "record"; Hugging Face at 100k models vs 1M). The earliest version of each story was kept.

Each decision is reversible: `git mv archive/posts/<file> content/posts/<file>` restores an article.

## What was kept

For duplicate clusters the earliest article stayed published. Nothing else was edited.
