## Free Growth Hacks

### 1. **SEO (biggest lever)**
- Every article has meta description, keywords, structured data
- Sitemap auto-submitted to Google/Bing
- Fast loading (Vercel) = better rankings
- **Timeline:** 2-4 weeks for Google to start indexing

### 2. **Reddit (highest ROI)**
- Post to r/technology, r/programming, r/artificial
- Title: "[P] I built a site that generates 15 tech news articles daily using AI"
- **Do NOT spam** — 1 post per subreddit per day max

### 3. **Hacker News**
- Submit interesting articles (not all 15)
- Best times: 9-11 AM EST, 7-9 PM EST
- **1 front page post = 10,000+ visitors**

### 4. **Dev.to / Hashnode**
- Cross-post your best articles
- Add "Originally published at TechPulse" link
- **Free backlinks + developer audience**

### 5. **Twitter/X**
- Auto-tweet every article
- Use hashtags: #TechNews #AI #Programming #Startup
- **Retweets = exponential reach**

### 6. **Google Discover**
- Good images + engaging titles = Google Discover traffic
- **No setup needed** — just good content
