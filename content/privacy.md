## Overview

TechPulse ("we", "us") publishes technology news at **tech-blog-eight-blush.vercel.app** (the "Site"). This policy explains what information is collected when you visit, why, and the choices you have. We do not require an account to read anything on the Site.

## Information we collect

### Server logs

The Site is hosted on Vercel. Like most hosts, Vercel processes technical request data to serve and secure the Site, including IP address, browser type, referring page, requested URL, and timestamp. We do not use this data to build profiles of individual readers.

### Analytics

We do not load third-party analytics or advertising trackers unless you consent (see "Cookies and consent" below). We do not sell personal information.

### Email

If you contact us by email, we receive your address and the contents of your message, and we keep it only as long as needed to respond and to maintain a record of the correspondence.

## Cookies and consent

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. We use them only for advertising, and only after you choose "Accept" in the consent banner.

- **Rejecting** (or ignoring the banner) means no advertising cookies are set and no ad script is loaded.
- **Accepting** allows Google AdSense to load and to set cookies as described below.
- You can change your choice at any time using the **Cookie settings** link in the footer.

## Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to display advertising. When you consent:

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to the Site and/or other sites on the internet.
- Google uses the DoubleClick DART cookie for this purpose.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time:

- **Google Ads Settings:** https://adssettings.google.com
- **Google's partner sites policy:** https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- **Industry opt-out (US):** https://www.aboutads.info/choices/
- **Industry opt-out (EU):** https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/

Opting out does not remove ads; it makes them non-personalized.

### Visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

Where required, we ask for consent before any advertising cookie is set, and ads are served on the basis of that consent. You may withdraw consent at any time via **Cookie settings**. Withdrawal stops future advertising cookies; it does not affect the lawfulness of processing that already occurred.

### California residents

Under the CCPA/CPRA you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, to correct it, and to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising. Because personalized advertising may involve sharing identifiers with ad networks, you can exercise the opt-out by selecting **Reject** in the consent banner or **Cookie settings** in the footer. We do not knowingly sell personal information for money.

## Third-party links

Articles link to external sources and companies. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices. Visiting them is at your own discretion.

## Children

The Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Data retention and security

Server logs are retained by our host according to its standard retention policy. We keep email correspondence only as long as necessary. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how data is handled, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date. Material changes affecting advertising or consent will be reflected in the consent banner as well.
