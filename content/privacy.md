## About this policy

TechPulse ("we", "us") operates **www.nexivo.org.in** (the "Site"). This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit the Site or contact us. You do not need an account to read our articles.

## Information processed when you visit

### Hosting and server logs

The Site is hosted by Vercel. To deliver, protect, and troubleshoot the Site, the hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP address, browser and device information, requested URL, referring URL, and request timestamp. These records are handled under the provider's own terms and retention practices.

### Email correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information in your message. We use it to respond, investigate corrections, and handle the request. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary.

### Analytics

We do not currently operate a third-party analytics or advertising measurement service on the Site.

## Cookies and advertising

The Site's AdSense publisher verification metadata and ads.txt record may be present for account and site verification. **Advertising requests and ad placements are currently disabled while we complete the required consent-management configuration.** We do not claim that a consent banner is active.

If advertising is enabled in the future, we will update this policy and configure an appropriate Google-certified consent management platform for regions where Google requires one before serving ads or using advertising-related storage. Visitors will be given the choices and controls provided by that consent platform.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential browser or hosting functionality may affect how the Site works.

## Third-party websites

Articles may link to external websites, research, documentation, and companies. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for their data practices.

## Children

The Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

## Data retention and security

Hosting providers retain technical logs according to their own policies. We retain email correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to respond, handle corrections, and maintain appropriate records. We use HTTPS where supported. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information contained in an email conversation or to request correction or deletion where applicable. We may need to retain information where required by law or for legitimate record-keeping.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Site's technology, services, or legal requirements change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.
