## About Nexivo

Nexivo is an independent technology site with hands-on guides to **AI engineering, automation, DevOps and security**. Almost everything here started as a project I built: the code is real, the commands were run, and the problems described are ones I actually hit.

## Who runs it

Hi, I'm **Jaiswar Abhishek**, a third-year B.Tech student at Parul University, and I build and run Nexivo on my own.

I started Nexivo as an experiment: can a student, working alone, build a technology site from scratch that real people actually find useful and come back to? So far that has meant learning far more than any one course covers: writing the templates in Hugo, setting up automated deployments on Vercel, working with the NIST vulnerability database API to build [Vulnerability Watch](/vuln-watch/), and, most of all, learning how to explain technical topics clearly.

I write about what I'm learning and building, mostly AI applications (embeddings, vector search, retrieval-augmented generation), automation pipelines, GitHub Actions, and the security of the code I write. When a guide shows steps or code, it's because I've run them myself. When I get something wrong, I want to know, so corrections are always welcome.

The whole site is open source. You can see how it's built, or borrow anything useful for your own projects, on [GitHub](https://github.com/Abhishek01239).

## What you will find here

- **Project write-ups.** How I built things like a ["chat with your PDF" app](/posts/rag-pdf-chat-app-architecture/), a [resume screener](/posts/resume-screener-seven-versions/) and a [self-tuning video pipeline](/posts/thompson-sampling-bandit-video-editing/), including the parts that went wrong.
- **Guides and explainers.** Focused explanations of one concept at a time, such as [chunking in RAG](/posts/rag-chunk-size-overlap-explained/), [CVSS scores](/posts/cvss-scores-explained/) or [GitHub Actions schedules](/posts/github-actions-cron-schedule-gotchas/), with runnable examples and their real output.
- **Not a news site.** Nexivo doesn't rewrite other outlets' reporting. Older news articles were retired in November 2026 when the site refocused on original, hands-on guides.
- **Free tools.** [Vulnerability Watch](/vuln-watch/) lists every critical and high-severity CVE published in the last 14 days, using data from the NIST National Vulnerability Database. It is free, needs no account, and does not track you.

## How articles are made

Every article on Nexivo is reviewed and approved by its author before it is published. AI tools may help with research, drafting and proofreading, but they are not treated as a source of facts: code is run, outputs shown in articles are real, and claims are checked against the primary sources linked at the end of each piece. The full process is in the [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).

## Corrections

If you spot an error, an outdated detail, or a broken link, [get in touch](/contact/) with the article URL and the issue. Corrections are prioritised, and significant changes are noted on the article.

## Independence

Nexivo is self-funded. Any advertising on the site is kept separate from editorial decisions, and sponsored content or affiliate links, if ever used, will be clearly labelled.
