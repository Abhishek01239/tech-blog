## About Nexivo

Nexivo is an independent technology site with practical guides and plain-English analysis on **cybersecurity, software development, and the cloud infrastructure** that modern products run on. It explains how things work and what they mean for the people who build and run software.

## Who runs it

Nexivo is written and maintained by **Abhishek Jaiswar**, a developer who builds the site, its tools, and its articles himself. The code for this website is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/Abhishek01239).

## What you will find here

- **Guides and explainers.** Step-by-step walkthroughs and explanations of security and developer concepts, written from hands-on use.
- **Analysis.** Selected security incidents, software releases, and infrastructure failures, with an explanation of what happened and what it means in practice. This is not a running news feed.
- **Free tools.** [Vulnerability Watch](/vuln-watch/) lists every critical and high-severity CVE published in the last 14 days, using data from the NIST National Vulnerability Database. It is free, needs no account, and does not track you.

## How articles are made

Every article on Nexivo is written or reviewed by its author before it is published. AI tools may help with research and proofreading, but they are not treated as a source of facts. Claims are checked against the primary sources linked at the end of each piece. The full process is in the [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).

## Corrections

If you spot an error, an outdated detail, or a broken link, [get in touch](/contact/) with the article URL and the issue. Corrections are prioritised, and significant changes are noted on the article.

## Independence

Nexivo is self-funded. Any advertising on the site is kept separate from editorial decisions, and sponsored content or affiliate links, if ever used, will be clearly labelled.
