These guides come from AI projects I have built myself: a retrieval-augmented "chat with your PDF" app, a resume screener that went through seven versions, and the experiments I ran to understand why they behaved the way they did. Each one includes the real code, the output it produced, and the mistakes worth avoiding, such as embeddings that silently truncate long text or similarity scores that are really distances.
