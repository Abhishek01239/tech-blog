## Introduction to NovaTech's Funding Round
NovaTech, a leading AI startup, has successfully raised $100 million in its latest funding round, led by prominent venture capital firms, Andreessen Horowitz and Sequoia Capital. This significant investment marks a major milestone for the company, which has been at the forefront of AI innovation since its inception in 2018. The funding round, announced on February 10, 2023, will enable NovaTech to further develop its AI-powered products and expand its team of experts.

## What Happened
The funding round was oversubscribed, with several top-tier investors participating, including Tiger Global and Coatue. NovaTech's CEO, Rachel Kim, expressed her gratitude to the investors, stating, 'We are thrilled to have the support of such esteemed investors, who share our vision of revolutionizing the AI landscape.' The company plans to utilize the funds to enhance its research and development capabilities, particularly in the areas of natural language processing and computer vision.

## Why It Matters
NovaTech's funding round is a testament to the growing interest in AI technology and its potential to transform various industries.
