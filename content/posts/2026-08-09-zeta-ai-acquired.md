# Zeta AI Acquired in Major Deal
Zeta AI, a leading artificial intelligence startup, has been acquired by a prominent tech firm in a deal worth $150 million. This significant acquisition is set to propel the development of AI technology, enhancing its applications across various sectors. The acquisition was finalized on February 10, 2024.

## What Happened
The acquisition of Zeta AI by TechCorp, a well-established player in the tech industry, marks a substantial milestone in the startup's journey. Founded in 2018 by a team of AI enthusiasts, Zeta AI has been at the forefront of AI innovation, focusing on natural language processing and machine learning. With this acquisition, TechCorp aims to integrate Zeta AI's cutting-edge technology into its existing portfolio, bolstering its position in the competitive AI market.
