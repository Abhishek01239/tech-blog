## What it does

You give DistilBook a PDF (scanned pages included), a Word document, Markdown, plain text, images, an article link or just a written prompt. It writes a narration script, then stops so you can read and edit every line. Only after you approve the script does it draw original artwork for each slide and animate it in time with the voice-over.

That approval step is the part worth noticing. Narration is locked once generation starts, so the review is where you fix names, numbers and wording. You can also write visual instructions to steer the artwork.

## How the videos look

DistilBook says every scene is drawn for your content and revealed stroke by stroke as the narration reaches it, rather than assembled from stock footage, synthetic presenters or template characters. There are 19 visual styles, a set of hand-drawn whiteboard looks plus motion-graphics styles such as isometric illustration, soft 3D, cut paper collage, inkwash journal and editorial flat vector.

Videos come in 16:9 landscape or 9:16 vertical, so the same document can become a course video and a short for social media. Narration is available in 20 languages, and your logo and brand colours are applied to every scene on every paid plan.

## How long it takes

According to the company, the script is ready to review within a couple of minutes. After you approve it, the first narrated slide plays in about 4 to 6 minutes, a short whiteboard video finishes in about 5 minutes, and a 10-slide video takes roughly 15 to 25 minutes.

A slide costs 10 credits, so the free credits cover the first slide of a video: you see the full narration, hear it and watch that slide in full. The rest unlock on a paid plan, which also removes the watermark and exports MP4 up to 1440p. Free videos get a share link and a watermark. PDF length limits are 10 pages on the free and Basic plans, 30 on Pro and 999 on Max.

## Who it suits

The company points to lessons, onboarding, SOPs, compliance training, research papers, product guides and book summaries. Its site lists a mix of schools, universities and small businesses among its users, and claims 1,500+ creators and 2,800+ videos. Those figures come from DistilBook itself and we have not verified them.

## What to check before paying

- **Test with your own document.** The free slide shows the narration quality and drawing style on your actual material.
- **Read the script closely.** The approval step exists because AI narration can get a name or figure wrong, and it cannot be changed after generation starts.
- **Count your credits.** At 10 credits a slide, the Basic plan's 230 credits cover about 23 slides a month.
- **Check the data terms.** The company says uploads stay in private storage tied to your account and are not used to train AI models.

## The bottom line

DistilBook is a specific tool for a common problem: material that deserves an audience but is too dense to read. If you produce training or course content regularly, the script-approval step and the free first slide make it cheap to find out whether the output fits your standard. We have not tested it ourselves, so treat the details above as the vendor's description.
