## Microsoft Cybersecurity Incident Unveiled
A recent cybersecurity incident has hit Microsoft, with the company revealing that approximately 250 million records were exposed in a December data breach. This significant vulnerability discovery has sparked widespread concerns over data protection and highlights the growing need for robust cybersecurity measures. The breach occurred due to misconfigured security rules, allowing unauthorized access to Microsoft's customer support database.

## What Happened
The data breach, which took place between December 5 and 31, 2019, involved the exposure of sensitive information, including email addresses, IP addresses, and case details. Microsoft has confirmed that no personal or financial data was compromised during the incident. An investigation into the breach is currently underway, with the company working to strengthen its security protocols to prevent similar incidents in the future.
