Honeywell Technologies says it is launching Forge Plant Cognition at Dangote’s Lekki refinery in Nigeria, bringing an industrial AI system to a specific operating plant rather than announcing another general-purpose model. The platform is built with NVIDIA AI and accelerated-computing capabilities and Dell Technologies infrastructure. Honeywell says it is intended to help operators improve throughput and plant performance; its financial projections remain preliminary, not reported savings. [1] [2]

## What the system is meant to do

Honeywell describes Forge Plant Cognition as combining process-technology expertise, industrial automation and AI to identify constraints, anticipate problems and support decisions about plant operations. Chemical Processing reports that the system is intended to help engineers investigate production problems and assess operating adjustments. The public announcement does not provide measured post-deployment results or a quantified improvement in refinery output. [1] [3]

The first deployment is at Dangote’s refinery in Lekki, Lagos State. Honeywell identifies Dangote as its launch partner and says the project targets higher throughput while supporting the capabilities of local engineering and operations teams. Reuters also reports the platform is being introduced at the site and identifies NVIDIA and Dell as technology contributors. [1] [2]

## A projection, not a realized saving

Honeywell says preliminary estimates indicate potential operational benefit of more than 29 cents per barrel. At full-capacity throughput, it estimates that would amount to more than $60 million in gross operational benefit annually for this facility. Reuters describes the figure as preliminary and not guaranteed; neither source presents it as money already saved. The estimate is tied to full-capacity operation, not necessarily current throughput, and is gross rather than net of deployment costs. [1] [2]

The project sits alongside a larger refinery expansion. Reuters says Honeywell is helping Dangote with a plan to raise nameplate capacity from 650,000 barrels per day to 1.4 million barrels per day by 2028. That expansion target should not be confused with an AI-driven capacity increase: the sources do not attribute the expansion itself to Forge Plant Cognition. [2]

## Industrial AI enters a live process setting

The announcement moves AI into a process industry where decisions affect equipment, throughput and product yield. For now, the public evidence establishes a launch and intended functions, not independently verified performance gains. The $60 million figure is best treated as Honeywell’s conditional estimate pending operational results at the refinery. [1] [3]

## Why It Matters

Deploying at a live refinery puts industrial AI into a setting where recommendations affect equipment, throughput and product yield, a harder test than a pilot or a benchmark. The $60 million figure is a preliminary, gross estimate tied to full-capacity operation and is not presented as money already saved. Judging the platform will require post-deployment results that neither Honeywell nor Reuters has published.
