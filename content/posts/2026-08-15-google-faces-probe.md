# Regulatory News About Big Tech Companies
Google is facing a major antitrust probe over its dominance in search and advertising markets. The probe, which was announced on February 10, 2023, will investigate whether Google has abused its market position to stifle competition. 

## What Happened
The investigation is being led by the US Federal Trade Commission (FTC) and will examine Google's conduct in the search and advertising markets. The probe will focus on whether Google has used its dominant position to limit competition and innovation in these markets. 

Google has faced several antitrust probes in the past, but this one is significant because it has the potential to lead to major changes in the way the company operates. The probe is also seen as a test of the Biden administration's commitment to regulating big tech companies. 

## Why It Matters
The probe matters because it has the potential to impact the entire tech industry. If Google is found to have abused its market position, it could face significant fines and be forced to make major changes to its business practices. This could have a ripple effect throughout the industry, as other big tech companies may also face increased scrutiny. 

The probe is also significant because it highlights the growing concern about the power of big tech companies. There is a growing consensus that these companies have too much power and are using it to stifle competition and innovation.

The probe is still in its early stages, and it is unclear what the outcome will be. However, one thing is certain: the probe has the potential to have a major impact on the tech industry. As the investigation continues, we can expect to see more developments and updates.
